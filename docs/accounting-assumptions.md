# Accounting decisions where the spec is silent

Rules that `packages/core` implements where [PROJECT_SPEC.md](PROJECT_SPEC.md) is silent, ambiguous or in conflict with BUILD_PROMPTS. Tests pin each behaviour, so a change shows up as a failing test.

Status:

- **decided**: on 2026-10-29 the owner delegated these choices ("search for the best practices and choose"). Each follows the cited practice or the most conservative reading. The owner can override any row: change the row, then the code and its tests.
- **spec**: a rule the spec states; listed because the code depends on it.

Rules that change the schema or the spec's formulas are also written into the spec (§19 lists them).

## Returns (BUILD_PROMPTS 5.3)

A customer gives back part of a sale. Nothing in the sale is edited (CLAUDE.md "Data rules"): a return is its own append-only record, and everything it changes is derived.

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Status                |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| RT1 | A return is a **credit note against the sale**: a `sale_return` (date, reason, the lines and lengths given back, the credit, the refund) with a `return` stock move per line that puts the length back on its roll. The sale and its lines are never changed; what the invoice is worth now is its total less the credits of its live returns. The cost put back on the roll is the **same share of the sale line's cost as of its length** (cumulative, half up, the last return takes the exact remainder, like the last sale off a roll), derived from the roll ledger, never typed.              | The append-only rule (corrections are new records); the cost must come back so stock at cost stays exact (T2, T3). Cumulative proportions never drift, in any number or order of returns.                                                                                       | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT2 | The **credit** is the proportional price of the length given back: `round(line total × returned ÷ sold)` per line (cumulative, the whole line exactly), and the invoice discount is shared in proportion to what is kept, so the customer gets back what they paid for those goods. There is no restocking fee and the credit cannot be typed: it is computed (@fabric/core `returnCreditCents`).                                                                                                                                                                                                    | SPEC §3 sells at a negotiated price per line; the discount is on the invoice (§4). A fee would be a rule the owner has not asked for.                                                                                                                                           | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT3 | Money: the credit first **lowers what the customer owes** on the invoice. Only what the customer has paid **beyond the invoice's new worth** is the shop's debt to them: it is handed back in cash now (a `refund` payment, up to that amount) or **stays as the customer's credit** on their account (refunded later, or applied to another open invoice, P6, P17). A walk-in buyer has no account to keep credit on: the whole overpayment goes back in cash. Allocations never exceed an invoice's worth: the excess is credit again (`effectiveAllocations`, latest allocations give way first). | P6, P13: the customer account already derives its credit from payments, refunds and allocations. An invoice cannot hold more than it is worth, and the over-applied money must not vanish nor appear twice in the integrity check.                                              | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT4 | **Profit follows the goods back, on the day of the return**: the profit an invoice's payments recognise is a function of its payments, its worth and its capital (capital first, S-rules). A return changes the worth and the capital, so what the same payments recognise changes; the **difference is booked on the return's date** as `returns_effect` (signed), never in a settled period. The below-cost loss of an invoice is reversed the same way. A kept-goods invoice that is now fully paid can raise the period, so the line is signed.                                                  | "Reverse profit recognition proportionally" (BUILD_PROMPTS 5.3). A settled period is locked (S7); the cost frozen at its settlement is the sale's cost before returns, and the return takes its share of that frozen cost, so a later measurement cannot change a closed month. | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT5 | A return is **dated in the open period** like any record (`periodDatesOf`): its date must be after the last settled day. The sale it returns may be in a settled period. A return dated before its sale is refused.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | The lock protects settled figures; the effect of the return is the open period's (RT4).                                                                                                                                                                                         | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT6 | A **voided** sale or a **written-off** debt takes no returns. A return cannot be larger than what was sold less what was already given back, line by line; two devices that return the same cloth offline are not both accepted (the second is rejected).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | The void already puts everything back (P13); a write-off already booked the loss of the unrecovered capital (S5), so returning goods against it would need that loss re-opened.                                                                                                 | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT7 | A return is **final** (append-only, like a void), and a sale with returns **cannot be voided**: the rest is returned instead, which has the same effect with its own audit trail. A mistaken return is corrected by selling the cloth again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | A void's reversals put the whole sold length back; after a partial return they would put it back twice.                                                                                                                                                                         | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT8 | "Restore stock to the roll or mark remnant": the length always goes back on its roll. The owner may also **mark that roll a remnant** by hand (the existing `roll.status`), for a returned piece that is no longer part of a whole roll. Returns in sales and reports are net: the month of the return, not of the sale, loses the sales and gross profit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | SPEC §4 remnants; reports must not count goods that came back as sold.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | decided; built in 5.3 |

A settlement that was unlocked keeps showing them, marked _unlocked_.                                                                                                                                                                  | S11 and S15: a rule or record changed later must not move a settled month, and the reader must be able to see what was approved.                                           | decided |
| SW6 | The **profit-split rules are managed in Partners** (`/settings/partners`), not in Settlement. Settlement shows the rule in effect on the period's first day (S3), whether it is the default, and a link. The conditions of S15 (future dates only, one live rule per date, voiding) are unchanged.                                                                                                                         | Partners already owns who the participants are and what they hold; the rules say how profit reaches them. One place to change them, and the settlement can only read.      | decided |
| SW7 | The **approval confirmation** shows the server's figures (every device's records, S16): period, recognised profit, the adjustments' total, net profit, each participant's share and the lock it makes. The button waits for the server's answer; a refusal stays in the dialog; a changed settlement (409) asks for a fresh preview; the dialog cannot be closed or the button pressed again while the request is pending. | The brief: never imply success before the server confirms, and never approve twice.                                                                                        | decided |

## People: partners and employees (T17, ADR 0022)

An employee is not a partner. A salary is the shop's expense, never a withdrawal by a partner and never a share of the profit.

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Status                |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| PY1 | **A salary payment is an ordinary expense.** It is an expense of the `wages` category (label "أجرة عامل") linked to the employee (`expense.employee_id`) and to the month it pays (`expense.salary_month`, `YYYY-MM`), dated the day it is paid and counted in the period of that paid date like any expense (S6). The settlement and the integrity check have **no second salary calculation**: they read the expense like any other. An employee has no profit share and no capital, takes no part in the split rule and never appears among the participants. Salaries are paid monthly; the payment method is not recorded.                                                                                                                                                    | One path for money that leaves the shop (E-rules, S6, S8), so a salary cannot be counted twice or missed, and a settled month stays readable. A partner's draw is a different thing (PA2): mixing them would hide who got what. | decided; built in T17 |
| PY2 | **What a month owes** is the monthly rate in force on the month's first day, in full: no proration, however late in the month the employee started or left. A rate of 0 stops the salary (leave, a pause). The month in which employment ended is owed in full; the months after it owe nothing. The month before the first rate owes nothing.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | The owner thinks in "so much a month". A proration rule (days worked, a 26-day month) is a labour-law choice the spec does not make; it can be added as a rule later without touching what is stored.                           | decided; built in T17 |
| PY3 | **What a month has been paid** is the sum of the live salary payments toward it (`salary_month`), whatever day each was paid, so an arrears payment or an advance counts toward the month it names. Owed = `max(0, due − paid)`, overpaid = `max(0, paid − due)`; the status is _none_ (nothing due, nothing paid), _unpaid_, _partial_ or _paid_. A payment that takes a month past what is due needs an explicit confirmation (`salary_payment_needs_confirmation`), never silent. Voiding a payment makes the month owe it again. The salary history lists the months from the first rate or payment through the month asked for, and a later month that was paid; a raise that has not begun adds no month of its own, because nothing is owed for a month that has not begun. | Paid and owed come from the records and are never entered (CLAUDE.md), so no balance can drift. A confirmation is how every over-the-limit write is allowed here (PA2).                                                         | decided; built in T17 |
| PY4 | **Rates are created or voided, never edited**, and each starts on the first day of a month. The first rate of an employee may start in any month from the month they started (back pay shows as owed); a later rate must start this month or later, so a raise never rewrites a month that is over. A rate that started in a month that is over cannot be voided; one that starts this month or later can, with a reason. A device that is offline checks a rate by the day it was typed; the server accepts the same rate when it syncs, and refuses one typed now for a month that has passed.                                                                                                                                                                                   | A salary already paid or owed for a past month must stay what it was (S11, SW5). The creation day is the fair test for work done offline.                                                                                       | decided; built in T17 |
| PY5 | **Who sees and changes people.** The owner changes employees, rates and salary payments; a partner reads them; a cashier never receives them: the API refuses a cashier (403) and the sync hub sends and accepts no `employee` or `employee_salary` record to or from the till.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | SPEC §11: a cashier sells and never sees the shop's cost or profit; a colleague's pay is the owner's business. Enforced on the server, never only in the screens.                                                               | decided; built in T17 |
| PY6 | **No employee with money in the books is removed.** One with a live salary payment is never voided: they are ended with an end date, and stay in the history. One who was never paid can be removed, with a reason (voided and audit-logged). Their rates stay with the record.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                    | CLAUDE.md: no hard delete, and cancellation is a compensating record; a payment must never point at a missing person.                                                                                                           | decided; built in T17 |
