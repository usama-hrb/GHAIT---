# Accounting decisions where the spec is silent

Rules that `packages/core` implements where [PROJECT_SPEC.md](PROJECT_SPEC.md) is silent, ambiguous or in conflict with BUILD_PROMPTS. Tests pin each behaviour, so a change shows up as a failing test.

Status:

- **decided**: on 2026-10-29 the owner delegated these choices ("search for the best practices and choose"). Each follows the cited practice or the most conservative reading. The owner can override any row: change the row, then the code and its tests.
- **spec**: a rule the spec states; listed because the code depends on it.

Rules that change the schema or the spec's formulas are also written into the spec (§19 lists them).

## Stock (BUILD_PROMPTS 3.3)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Why                                                                                                           | Status             |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------ |
| R1  | A roll's moves are costed in **history order**: the server's receipt order (`server_seq`), then the moves still only on the device, in the order they were made (`syncOrder`; until 2026-10-30, device creation time, which a wrong device clock could reorder). | R2  | A measurement can be entered at any time, and again later. Each one records the difference to the current basis (SPEC §2.3: what is on the roll now plus what was taken off). | R3  | A loss needs a reason and at least 1 cm; a stocktake needs a reason and a count that differs from the books. ## Sales (BUILD_PROMPTS 3.4)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Status                  |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------- |
| P8  | A sale needs an explicit confirmation (`confirmed_below_cost`) when a line or the invoice (after discount) is under cost (P2). The server prices it again on its own view of the rolls and refuses an unconfirmed one.                                                                                                                                                                             | SPEC §3; the server re-validates every write (CLAUDE.md). A sale priced differently on a device that was offline is rare and shows as a rejected op.                                                                                          | decided; spec §3        |
| P9  | A sale line stores no cost: its cost is the roll ledger's cost of its `sale` move, derived like a loss's (R1), so a measurement re-costs it without editing the line. Settling a period freezes its lines' costs (S14).                                                                                                                                                                            | SPEC §2.4 recomputes lines after a measurement while the period is open; a stored column would need rewriting, and two offline devices would store different values.                                                                          | decided; spec §2.4, §13 |
| P10 | A payment allocation stores its amount only. Its capital and profit parts are derived with `allocatePayment` from the sale's current capital, in the order of the sale's payments; once a settlement counts them, the sale's capital is frozen (S14), so they no longer change.                                                                                                                    | The parts must follow a measurement (T4's provisional profit), or the integrity check would be off by the difference; offline devices cannot agree on the capital when the payment is made. Locking keeps settled periods fixed (§8.2's aim). | decided; spec §4.1, §13 |
| P11 | The POS proposes the **oldest roll that holds the whole length** (purchase date, then creation time, then code). When none does, it takes the rolls oldest first, each to its end. Remnants count as stock. The device never sells more than its rolls hold (a kg roll that turns out longer is measured first); a sale that oversells after an offline sync is accepted and flagged (`oversold`). | SPEC §4 ("أقدم رول فيه ما يكفي"); FIFO is the natural order for the split.                                                                                                                                                                    | decided; spec §4        |
| P12 | What is paid at the counter is cash, at most the total. Less than the total is a credit sale and needs a customer; a due date only on credit, not before the sale. It is recorded as a payment applied to the sale, in the sale's op.                                                                                                                                                              | SPEC §4.1: paid is derived from payments; a walk-in buyer has no account to owe on.                                                                                                                                                           | decided                 |
| P13 | Voiding a sale writes a `sale_void` (reason required, audited) and a `reversal` move per line that cancels the line's move: the roll is costed as if the line had never been made. What was paid on the sale is refunded (`payment.kind = refund`). One void per sale.                                                                                                                             | Append-only moves and payments (CLAUDE.md); a void is a compensating record. Refunding is the default for a cancelled sale; keeping it as the customer's credit can come with 3.5.                                                            | decided                 |

Percentages still work as weights (40/40/20).                                                 | Fowler's money pattern allocates by ratios with integer cents, which is what this is. ## Settlement and the period lock (BUILD_PROMPTS 4.3)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Why                                                                                                                                                                                              | Status                  |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ----------------------- |
| S7  | Periods are **contiguous**: a settlement runs from the day after the last one (the first takes everything before it) to the end the owner picks, usually a month's last day. It is settled once that day has passed (`end < today`), never before.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | No record falls between two periods or in two of them; a day is settled only when its sales are all in.                                                                                          | decided                 |
| S8  | What a period counts (`periodResultOf`): the profit part of each payment **applied** in it (parts derived capital first in the order applied, P10); a below-cost invoice's loss in the period of its **sale** (P3); damage at cost on the **loss** date; expenses by **paid** date (S6). A voided sale counts nothing (its payments became credit and were refunded, P13).                                                                                                             | SPEC §8.1, §8.2, as the integrity check counts them. Splitting time into more periods moves amounts between them, never changes their total (a property test).                                   | decided                 |
| S9  | A bad debt (S5) loses the invoice's collectible capital less what payments **dated up to the write-off** recovered. Capital recovered by later payments is **recovered bad debt**: a gain of their period, its own line (`bad_debt_recovered`, settlement column `recovered_bad_debt_cents`); their profit part counts as profit.                                                                                                                                                      | A recovery reverses a loss already settled: shown as a gain, never a negative loss. Over the invoice's life the total is its profit less what was never recovered.                               | decided; spec §8.1, §13 |
| S10 | The shop's third is `retained_profit` dated on the period's **last day**, linked to its settlement (`capital_move.settlement_id`). It is never entered or voided by hand: it goes only when its settlement is unlocked (PA3).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | It belongs to the period it came from; the link lets the unlock remove exactly what the settlement created.                                                                                      | decided                 |
| S11 | **The lock**: a record whose accounting date is on or before the last settled day cannot be created, changed or voided. The date is the record's own: a sale, payment, allocation, write-off, stock move, purchase, capital move, draw by its date; an expense by its paid date (unpaid: in no period, so paying it later counts in the open period); a sale void by its date **and** its sale's. Master data (fabrics, customers, settings, templates) is not dated, so never locked. | SPEC §8.3 "بعد التسوية تُقفل"; CLAUDE.md "Records inside a locked settlement period change only through an explicit, audit-logged unlock". Lock dates are how accounting systems close a period. | decided                 |
| S12 | A record dated in a settled period that reaches the server **after** the settlement (made offline before it) is **refused** (`period_locked`), not moved to the open period. It stays visible on its device; the owner unlocks the period (with the reason), enters it again and settles again.                                                                                                                                                                                        | Moving it silently would put October's sale in November's figures. Refusing keeps every settled figure exact, and the case is rare: approval tells the owner to sync every device first.         | decided                 |
| S13 | **Unlocking**: only the latest live settlement (a later one carried its deficit), with a reason in the audit log. It voids the settlement with its shares, retained profit and frozen costs; the period is then open and is settled again as any other.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | SPEC §8.3 "التعديل بإجراء ظاهر ومسجَّل". Voiding, never deleting (CLAUDE.md).                                                                                                                    | decided                 |
| S14 | **Frozen costs** (`locked_cost`): approving a settlement freezes the cost of every sale and loss move it counted, and of the sales whose payments or write-off it counted. Later measurements re-cost only the moves not frozen (P9, R1). Unlocking releases them.                                                                                                                                                                                                                     | A kg roll measured in November must not change October's settled profit; the roll's total cost still holds exactly (the moves not frozen share the rest).                                        | decided                 |
| S15 | **Split rules**: entered, changed or voided only while their date is **after today**, one live rule per date. The default, equal thirds, is in force since `2000-01-01` and is not a record. A settlement keeps the weights it was settled with.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | SPEC §8.3: never retroactive; with S3 a rule never applies to a period that has started. Keeping the weights makes an old settlement readable whatever rules come later.                         | decided                 |
| S16 | **Approval is online**: the server settles on every device's records and refuses if the figures differ from the breakdown the owner approved (`settlement_preview_changed`). Devices preview offline from their own records.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | Only the server has every device's records; a settlement approved on one device's partial view would be wrong for good.                                                                          | decided; ADR 0005       |

## Integrity check (BUILD_PROMPTS 1.5, 4.4)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                    | Status                 |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| I1  | Overpayments the shop holds for customers are a **claim** in the check, next to capital, partner balances and unsettled profit; money paid beyond a written-off invoice too.                                                                                                                                                                                         | SPEC §9 leaves them out, so every overpayment would show as a difference of its amount. The money belongs to the customer (P6).                                        | decided; spec §9       |
| I2  | A `cash_adjustment` (counted − expected, signed) is **cash over/short of the period of the count**: it changes the cash and the period's result alike (`cashOverShortCents` in the settlement, its own breakdown line).                                                                                                                                              | The standard "cash over and short" account: a shortage is an expense, an overage income. Without it every cash count would show as a difference.                       | decided; spec §8.1, §9 |
| I3  | A partner draw is cash out of the till; a personal expense the shop pays for a partner is a draw too. Fabric a partner takes for themselves is a draw **at cost**: stock goes down by its cost, their balance by the same.                                                                                                                                           | SPEC §8.4 "نقد أو مصروف شخصي". An owner's draw of inventory is recorded at cost; at selling price it would book a profit nobody paid for.                              | decided; built in 4.2  |
| I4  | The check holds with supplier debts: what the shop owes its suppliers is a **claim** next to capital and customer credits (SP1). A purchase paid in full adds nothing to it.                                                                                                                                                                                         | SPEC §9 assumed no supplier debts in phase 1; 5.3 adds them, so a part-paid purchase (stock in, cash out only in part) balances.                                       | decided; built in 5.3  |
| I5  | A cash count is of **all the shop's money**: the till plus the shop's bank account. The records do not split them: customer payments carry a method, but purchases, expenses, capital moves and draws do not. The count screen shows what customers paid by bank transfer, to help reconcile.                                                                        | PA4: the shop's money counts payments of every method. Splitting till and bank would need a method on every money record, a later change.                              | decided; built in 4.4  |
| I6  | A count compares what was counted with the **device's** books when it is entered, and keeps both (`expected_cents`, `diff_cents`, as SPEC §13 has them). It is never edited: a wrong count is voided (with a reason) or counted again. Records another device syncs later move the books after the count; the next count corrects it. The screen asks to sync first. | Counting is a daily, offline operation; a count is a snapshot, like the journal entry it stands for. Any error it carries shows up and is corrected at the next count. | decided; built in 4.4  |
| I7  | The customer credits in the check: per customer, money paid in less refunds less what was applied to live invoices (never below zero), plus any invoice paid beyond its total. Money applied to a voided sale is credit, refunded with the void (P13).                                                                                                               | P6, P14 in the check's terms; per customer, so one customer's over-applied money never hides another's credit.                                                         | decided; built in 4.4  |
| I8  | The check's unsettled net profit is everything after the last settlement, future-dated records included, with its carried deficit: what the next settlement will count (periodResultOf).                                                                                                                                                                             | Every record is in the books once entered; leaving future-dated ones out would show them as a difference.                                                              | decided; built in 4.4  |

The check catches records that disagree with each other: a roll whose cost no longer matches its purchase, a record changed inside a settled period, a settlement or capital move that does not match. A record that is consistently missing (a sale never entered) balances; only the physical cash count shows it.

## Returns (BUILD_PROMPTS 5.3)

A customer gives back part of a sale. Nothing in the sale is edited (CLAUDE.md "Data rules"): a return is its own append-only record, and everything it changes is derived.

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                             | Status                |
| --- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| RT1 | A return is a **credit note against the sale**: a `sale_return` (date, reason, the lines and lengths given back, the credit, the refund) with a `return` stock move per line that puts the length back on its roll. The sale and its lines are never changed; what the invoice is worth now is its total less the credits of its live returns. The cost put back on the roll is the **same share of the sale line's cost as of its length** (cumulative, half up, the last return takes the exact remainder, like the last sale off a roll), derived from the roll ledger, never typed.              | The append-only rule (corrections are new records); the cost must come back so stock at cost stays exact (T2, T3). Cumulative proportions never drift, in any number or order of returns.                                                                                       | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT2 | The **credit** is the proportional price of the length given back: `round(line total × returned ÷ sold)` per line (cumulative, the whole line exactly), and the invoice discount is shared in proportion to what is kept, so the customer gets back what they paid for those goods. There is no restocking fee and the credit cannot be typed: it is computed (@fabric/core `returnCreditCents`).                                                                                                                                                                                                    | SPEC §3 sells at a negotiated price per line; the discount is on the invoice (§4). A fee would be a rule the owner has not asked for.                                                                                                                                           | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT3 | Money: the credit first **lowers what the customer owes** on the invoice. Only what the customer has paid **beyond the invoice's new worth** is the shop's debt to them: it is handed back in cash now (a `refund` payment, up to that amount) or **stays as the customer's credit** on their account (refunded later, or applied to another open invoice, P6, P17). A walk-in buyer has no account to keep credit on: the whole overpayment goes back in cash. Allocations never exceed an invoice's worth: the excess is credit again (`effectiveAllocations`, latest allocations give way first). | P6, P13: the customer account already derives its credit from payments, refunds and allocations. An invoice cannot hold more than it is worth, and the over-applied money must not vanish nor appear twice in the integrity check.                                              | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT4 | **Profit follows the goods back, on the day of the return**: the profit an invoice's payments recognise is a function of its payments, its worth and its capital (capital first, S-rules). A return changes the worth and the capital, so what the same payments recognise changes; the **difference is booked on the return's date** as `returns_effect` (signed), never in a settled period. The below-cost loss of an invoice is reversed the same way. A kept-goods invoice that is now fully paid can raise the period, so the line is signed.                                                  | "Reverse profit recognition proportionally" (BUILD_PROMPTS 5.3). A settled period is locked (S7); the cost frozen at its settlement is the sale's cost before returns, and the return takes its share of that frozen cost, so a later measurement cannot change a closed month. | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT5 | A return is **dated in the open period** like any record (`periodDatesOf`): its date must be after the last settled day. The sale it returns may be in a settled period. A return dated before its sale is refused.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | The lock protects settled figures; the effect of the return is the open period's (RT4).                                                                                                                                                                                         | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT6 | A **voided** sale or a **written-off** debt takes no returns. A return cannot be larger than what was sold less what was already given back, line by line; two devices that return the same cloth offline are not both accepted (the second is rejected).                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | The void already puts everything back (P13); a write-off already booked the loss of the unrecovered capital (S5), so returning goods against it would need that loss re-opened.                                                                                                 | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT7 | A return is **final** (append-only, like a void), and a sale with returns **cannot be voided**: the rest is returned instead, which has the same effect with its own audit trail. A mistaken return is corrected by selling the cloth again.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                         | A void's reversals put the whole sold length back; after a partial return they would put it back twice.                                                                                                                                                                         | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RT8 | "Restore stock to the roll or mark remnant": the length always goes back on its roll. The owner may also **mark that roll a remnant** by hand (the existing `roll.status`), for a returned piece that is no longer part of a whole roll. Returns in sales and reports are net: the month of the return, not of the sale, loses the sales and gross profit.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                           | SPEC §4 remnants; reports must not count goods that came back as sold.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | decided; built in 5.3 |

## Supplier payables (BUILD_PROMPTS 5.3)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                        | Why                                                                                                                           | Status                |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| SP1 | What the shop owes a supplier is **Σ purchases − Σ paid at purchase − Σ payments**, derived (@fabric/core `supplierAccount`), never stored. It is a **claim** in the integrity check (a negative balance, an advance, nets against the others). A purchase part paid needs a supplier.                                                                      | SPEC §13 `purchase.paid_cents`; the check's two sides: stock came in at its full cost, cash went out only for what was paid.  | decided; built in 5.3 |
| SP2 | Per purchase, a payment names the purchase it pays first (the rest passing on) or none; payments without a target and the rest pay the **oldest purchases first**. The supplier's balance does not depend on this order, only the dues shown per purchase.                                                                                                  | The statement must say which purchase is still owed; the totals must not depend on it.                                        | decided; built in 5.3 |
| SP3 | A `supplier_payment` is created, or voided with a reason, never edited; it is **limited to what is owed** (an advance is an asset nobody asked for, and a slip shows as a refusal). It leaves the shop's cash (`supplierPaymentsCents` in the cash flows), is dated for the period lock, and is not a loss or an expense: it is not part of the settlement. | The payment settles a debt that came with the stock (stock at cost); the profit was and is recognised on the sales (S-rules). | decided; built in 5.3 |
| SP4 | The **zakat base** subtracts what the shop owes suppliers, with the money held for customers: "الديون الحالّة على المحل (الموردون وغيرهم)". A supplier's advance is not an asset in the base. Reports saved before this keep their items (`supplierDebts` defaults to none).                                                                                | SPEC §10 states it; Z2.                                                                                                       | decided; built in 5.3 |
| SP5 | A partner paying a supplier from their own pocket (SPEC §17.5) is **not built**: it would be a debt of the shop to the partner, a new claim. Until then the owner records the purchase paid, or pays the supplier from the shop's cash.                                                                                                                     | Still open in the spec; no guess.                                                                                             | open                  |

## Roles (BUILD_PROMPTS 5.3)

SPEC §11: the owner; a partner who sees the same numbers; a worker who sells and does not see cost or profit. Enforced on the API; the screens only hide what it would refuse (ADR 0007).

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Why                                                                                                                                                                                               | Status                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| RL1 | A **cashier** never receives a cost or the partners' money: the sync hub sends its device only what selling needs (catalog, rolls **with their cost set to 0**, stock moves, customers, sales, payments, returns, voids and three settings). Purchases, suppliers, expenses, partners, settlements, zakat, counts and the audit log are not sent. | A device keeps a copy of everything it is sent, so hiding a column in the UI would not hide it. The table is `CASHIER_ACCESS` in @fabric/contracts: the compiler refuses a new entity not listed. | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RL2 | A cashier reaches **no REST route** except the sync hub, device registration and its own profile (`@CashierMayCall`); every other route answers 403. A partner reads every route and writes none (4.2); the owner everything.                                                                                                                     | Default-deny: a new route is closed to a cashier until someone opens it on purpose.                                                                                                               | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RL3 | A cashier **pushes** only sales, customer records and customer payments. It cannot void, return, refund, write off, buy, spend, edit the catalog or a roll (a roll edit from its redacted copy would overwrite the cost), or **confirm a sale under cost**. The device refuses the same changes before saving.                                    | Cash does not leave the till as a refund without the owner; a cost-blind till cannot weigh a loss.                                                                                                | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RL4 | Because the cashier's device sees cost 0, it cannot warn about a sale under cost. The **server prices every sale with the real costs**; a sale under cost without the owner's confirmation is **rejected** and shows in the sync issues for the owner. The till still warns under the minimum price (not a cost).                                 | The minimum price is a selling rule the cashier may know; the cost is not. The owner decides what to do with the sale that happened at the counter.                                               | decided; built in 5.3 |
| RL5 | Accounts: `make cashier-user USERNAME=…` creates one (or resets its password); there can be several. Roles are not changed from the app.                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Like `make owner` and `make partner-user`: the password never touches argv or the shell history.                                                                                                  | decided; built in 5.3 |

## Zakat (BUILD_PROMPTS 1.5, 5.1)

A calculation tool, not a fatwa: these are settings or readings for the owner to check with a trusted scholar (SPEC §10, §17.1).

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                  | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Status                |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------- |
| Z1  | Goods at market value = remaining length × the reference price per metre, rounded half up per roll. A ready piece is valued per metre too, not at its piece price. At cost = the roll's stock value at cost.                                                                                                                          | SPEC §10: "بسعر السوق (السعر المرجعي للرول)".                                                                                                                                                                         | decided               |
| Z2  | Receivables count at the full amount owed (profit included). Debts due on the shop include customer credits. Undrawn partner profit is not deducted: it is the partners' own money in the shop.                                                                                                                                       | SPEC §10 base: "الديون المرجوّ تحصيلها" and "الديون الحالّة على المحل (الموردون وغيرهم)".                                                                                                                             | decided               |
| Z3  | A base equal to the nisab reaches it. Zakat is rounded half up to the cent. The rate is fixed at 2.5% (the hawl is Hijri).                                                                                                                                                                                                            | SPEC §10 "إذا بلغ النصاب"; §0 rounding.                                                                                                                                                                               | decided               |
| Z4  | The shop is a **managing merchant** ("مدير"): zakat every hawl on stock, cash and expected debts. The "محتكر" option is not built.                                                                                                                                                                                                    | In the Maliki school the مدير is the shopkeeper who sells every day; the محتكر holds goods waiting for a better price and pays once, after selling and receiving the price. A fabric shop selling daily is the first. | decided               |
| Z5  | The doubtful debts (listed apart, counted only if the owner chooses) are what is still due on **written-off** invoices: the owner already judged them unlikely to be paid.                                                                                                                                                            | SPEC §10 "المشكوك فيها في خانة منفصلة يقرر المالك"; the write-off (P16) is that judgment.                                                                                                                             | decided; built in 5.1 |
| Z6  | A report is for the **latest hawl** on or before today, computed from the records when it is saved (save it on the hawl day; the reminder comes two weeks before, and stays until it is saved). One live report per hawl; none before the first hawl.                                                                                 | A snapshot "as of the hawl" would need every balance replayed to a past date; the reminder makes the two days the same in practice.                                                                                   | decided; built in 5.1 |
| Z7  | Paying zakat records **one draw per partner by ownership** (largest remainder, odd cent to the owner), linked to the report (`partner_draw.zakat_report_id`). What a report has been paid is derived from its live draws: there is no `paid_cents` column (SPEC §13). Never beyond what is due; a report with payments is not voided. | SPEC §10: "تُخصم من حساب كل شريك بنسبة ملكيته، وليست مصروفاً". A draw is how the shop pays a partner's own obligation (I3); a derived total never disagrees with its payments (CLAUDE.md).                            | decided; built in 5.1 |
| Z8  | Hawls fall on the **Hijri** day and month of the first hawl's start (Umm al-Qura calendar, as `Intl` computes it), each Hijri year; a start on the 30th falls on the 29th in a month without one.                                                                                                                                     | SPEC §10 "بالتقويم الهجري"; Umm al-Qura is the calendar browsers and Node ship.                                                                                                                                       | decided; built in 5.1 |
| Z9  | Zakat settings (nisab metal, valuation, doubtful debts, hawl start, metal prices with their dates, reminder days) are shop settings, changed online like the others; each report keeps the settings and the price it used.                                                                                                            | SPEC §0.7 settings live on the server; the snapshot keeps an old report readable whatever the settings become.                                                                                                        | decided; built in 5.1 |
| Z10 | A zakat report is a snapshot, not a record of the books: it is not period-locked, so a hawl's report can be saved after its month is settled. Its payments (draws) are locked by their date. The report is shared as WhatsApp text, not printed (CLAUDE.md).                                                                          | The lock protects settled figures; a report changes none.                                                                                                                                                             | decided; built in 5.1 |
