# Accounting decisions where the spec is silent

Rules that `packages/core` implements where [PROJECT_SPEC.md](PROJECT_SPEC.md) is silent, ambiguous or in conflict with BUILD_PROMPTS. Tests pin each behaviour, so a change shows up as a failing test.

Status:

- **decided**: on 2026-10-29 the owner delegated these choices ("search for the best practices and choose"). Each follows the cited practice or the most conservative reading. The owner can override any row: change the row, then the code and its tests.
- **spec**: a rule the spec states; listed because the code depends on it.

Rules that change the schema or the spec's formulas are also written into the spec (§19 lists them).

## Purchases (BUILD_PROMPTS 3.2)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              | Why                                                                                                                                           | Status  |
| --- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| B1  | The renewal warning (SPEC §8.5) compares the new purchase's average cost per metre with the **previous purchase of the same fabric** (Σ cost ÷ Σ initial length of its rolls), exactly, by cross-multiplication. A kg roll counts with its estimated length until it is measured. | "متوسط آخر دفعة": the last batch is the last purchase. Comparing exactly avoids a false alarm or a missed one caused by rounding to the cent. | decided |
| B2  | A purchase is paid in full when recorded (`paid_cents = total_cost_cents`, also a database CHECK).                                                                                                                                                                                | SPEC §9 / I4: no supplier debts in phase 1; BUILD_PROMPTS 5.3 adds supplier payables.                                                         | spec    |
| B3  | A purchase cannot be edited once saved; correcting one waits for the stock-correction screens (3.3), as compensating stock moves.                                                                                                                                                 | Append-only rolls and moves (CLAUDE.md); a purchase's costs already feed later sales.                                                         | decided |

## Pricing and payments (BUILD_PROMPTS 1.3)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                | Status                 |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| P1  | Line total = `round(price per metre × sold cm / 100)`, half up (18.05 dh/m × 2.55 m → 46.03 dh).                                                                                                                                                                                                   | A price in rial or dirham times a length in cm is not always a whole number of cents.                                                                                                                                                              | decided                |
| P2  | The below-cost loss is measured **per invoice**: `loss = max(0, capital − total)`. A below-cost line inside a profitable invoice still needs confirmation, but only lowers that invoice's profit.                                                                                                  | SPEC §8.2 defines capital/profit per invoice and §8.1 counts the "negative part" as a loss. Counting line losses as well as the net invoice profit would count them twice.                                                                         | decided                |
| P3  | That loss belongs to the **period of the sale**, and payments recover at most `min(capital, total)`.                                                                                                                                                                                               | Waiting for payment would leave the integrity check out of balance until then (the check proves it); a fully paid below-cost invoice has nothing more to collect.                                                                                  | decided                |
| P4  | An invoice discount lowers the invoice's profit part only; line profits (for reports) ignore it.                                                                                                                                                                                                   | §8.2 works on the invoice total. Spreading discounts over lines for per-fabric reports is a reporting choice (BUILD_PROMPTS 5.2).                                                                                                                  | decided                |
| P5  | A ready piece is sold "whole" only while untouched. Once cut, the rest is sold by the metre.                                                                                                                                                                                                       | The piece price is for the full piece (SPEC §2.3).                                                                                                                                                                                                 | decided                |
| P6  | An overpayment is never income: it is a **credit held for the customer** (a claim in the integrity check). It first goes to that customer's other open invoices, oldest first (the general-payment rule). What remains is offered at their next sale, or refunded in cash with a refund record.    | Standard receivables practice: unapplied cash is a customer credit, a liability until applied or refunded. Reusing the §4.1 rule keeps one allocation path.                                                                                        | decided; built in 3.5  |
| P7  | Payments are applied through an append-only **`payment_allocation`** table (`payment_id`, `sale_id`, `amount_cents`, `capital_cents`, `profit_cents`), written when the payment is recorded. `payment.sale_id` goes; a payment's unapplied rest (amount − Σ allocations) is the customer's credit. | Receivables systems keep payments and their applications apart (one payment ↔ many invoices). Storing the capital/profit split at the time keeps settled periods from ever being recomputed (§8.2), and a later edit shows in the integrity check. | decided; schema in 2.1 |

## Settlement (BUILD_PROMPTS 1.4)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                          | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            | Status                 |
| --- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ---------------------- |
| S1  | The split rule is three integer **weights**, default **1/1/1** (`profit_split_rule.owner_weight / partner_weight / shop_weight`): owner and partner get `floor(net × weight / Σ weights)`, the shop gets the rest. Percentages still work as weights (40/40/20).              | Fowler's money pattern allocates by ratios with integer cents, which is what this is. Basis points cannot express a third: BUILD_PROMPTS 1.4's 3333/3333/3334 gives T6 9999/9999/10002 instead of the spec's 10000/10000/10000 (a test pins it). The spec's equal thirds and "division cents to the shop" win. | decided; spec §13      |
| S2  | The shop share becomes `retained_profit` split by ownership with the largest remainder; an odd cent on a 50/50 split goes to the **owner**.                                                                                                                                   | SPEC §8.1 says "half to each partner"; an odd shop share (3333) cannot be halved. Fowler's allocation also gives the leftover to the first in the list.                                                                                                                                                        | decided                |
| S3  | A period is settled with the rule in effect on its **first day**. A rule starting mid-period first applies to the next period. Two rules on the same date, or no rule in effect, is an error.                                                                                 | SPEC §8.3: no retroactive change of the split. BUILD_PROMPTS 4.3 seeds the default rule.                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | decided                |
| S4  | Exactly two partners, one owner, ownership adding up to 10000 bp. The profit split does not depend on ownership; ownership only splits the shop's retained third.                                                                                                             | SPEC §6, §8.1: the split rule is set on its own.                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                               | decided                |
| S5  | A bad debt loses its **unrecovered capital** (`unrecoveredCapitalCents`), booked in the period of the write-off. If the customer later pays a written-off invoice, the payment first counts as a recovery (a gain of its period, up to the loss written off), then as profit. | Its profit part was never recognised, so writing off the whole amount due would count it as a loss: the integrity check shows it as a difference (a test pins this). Recovering a written-off debt reverses the loss when the cash arrives.                                                                    | decided; built in 3.5  |
| S6  | An expense counts in the period of its **paid date**: `expense.paid_date`, null while unpaid. An unpaid expense counts in no period.                                                                                                                                          | Cash basis: an expense is recognised when the money leaves, which is what SPEC §5 "مدفوعة في الفترة" says. The schema had only `paid`, so an expense paid after its month was settled had no period to fall in.                                                                                                | decided; schema in 4.1 |

## Integrity check (BUILD_PROMPTS 1.5)

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                                       | Why                                                                                                                                              | Status                 |
| --- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ---------------------- |
| I1  | Overpayments the shop holds for customers are a **claim** in the check, next to capital, partner balances and unsettled profit.                                                                                            | SPEC §9 leaves them out, so every overpayment would show as a difference of its amount. The money belongs to the customer (P6).                  | decided; spec §9       |
| I2  | A `cash_adjustment` (counted − expected, signed) is **cash over/short of the period of the count**: it changes the cash and the period's result alike (`cashOverShortCents` in the settlement, its own breakdown line).    | The standard "cash over and short" account: a shortage is an expense, an overage income. Without it every cash count would show as a difference. | decided; spec §8.1, §9 |
| I3  | A partner draw is cash out of the till; a personal expense the shop pays for a partner is a draw too. Fabric a partner takes for themselves is a draw **at cost**: stock goes down by its cost, their balance by the same. | SPEC §8.4 "نقد أو مصروف شخصي". An owner's draw of inventory is recorded at cost; at selling price it would book a profit nobody paid for.        | decided; built in 4.2  |
| I4  | The check holds only while purchases are paid in full.                                                                                                                                                                     | SPEC §9 assumes no supplier debts in phase 1; supplier debts become a claim in 5.3.                                                              | spec                   |

The check catches records that disagree with each other: a roll whose cost no longer matches its purchase, a record changed inside a settled period, a settlement or capital move that does not match. A record that is consistently missing (a sale never entered) balances; only the physical cash count shows it.

## Zakat (BUILD_PROMPTS 1.5)

A calculation tool, not a fatwa: these are settings or readings for the owner to check with a trusted scholar (SPEC §10, §17.1).

| #   | Rule                                                                                                                                                                                                         | Why                                                                                                                                                                                                                   | Status  |
| --- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ------- |
| Z1  | Goods at market value = remaining length × the reference price per metre, rounded half up per roll. A ready piece is valued per metre too, not at its piece price. At cost = the roll's stock value at cost. | SPEC §10: "بسعر السوق (السعر المرجعي للرول)".                                                                                                                                                                         | decided |
| Z2  | Receivables count at the full amount owed (profit included). Debts due on the shop include customer credits. Undrawn partner profit is not deducted: it is the partners' own money in the shop.              | SPEC §10 base: "الديون المرجوّ تحصيلها" and "الديون الحالّة على المحل (الموردون وغيرهم)".                                                                                                                             | decided |
| Z3  | A base equal to the nisab reaches it. Zakat is rounded half up to the cent. The rate is fixed at 2.5% (the hawl is Hijri).                                                                                   | SPEC §10 "إذا بلغ النصاب"; §0 rounding.                                                                                                                                                                               | decided |
| Z4  | The shop is a **managing merchant** ("مدير"): zakat every hawl on stock, cash and expected debts. The "محتكر" option is not built.                                                                           | In the Maliki school the مدير is the shopkeeper who sells every day; the محتكر holds goods waiting for a better price and pays once, after selling and receiving the price. A fabric shop selling daily is the first. | decided |
